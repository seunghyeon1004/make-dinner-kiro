**These two change how the brief reads, so check them first:**

1. **Prep-forward never moves terminal steps.** As the brief is written, the prep-forward pass would move a terminal step whose result keeps (a chilled dessert). That breaks G4.1, which says every terminal step ends at minute 0 when resources are unlimited. Right-justify may still move terminal steps.
2. **R3 applies only to pending steps.** A cook may start a step early, and R2 keeps that actual start, so "no step moves earlier than it was" cannot hold for pinned steps.

**Product and data**
