ROAST CHICKEN WITH LEMON AND THYME
(1 chicken, about 1.6 kg, feeds four)

Heat the oven to 200C, give it a good 15 minutes to come up. Meanwhile pat the bird dry, rub it all over with soft butter, salt and pepper, and push a halved lemon and a few thyme sprigs inside. That's about 10 minutes of work. Roast for 1 hour 15 minutes, until the juices run clear. Take it out and let it rest under foil for 20 minutes before carving (don't skip this, and it'll stay hot under the foil for half an hour anyway). Carving takes 5 minutes, do it right before it goes to the table.

Crispy roast potatoes

1. Peel 1 kg of floury potatoes and cut into big chunks - 10 min.
2. Boil in salted water for 10 minutes until the edges are soft. Drain, then shake the pan to rough them up (a couple of minutes).
3. Tip into a roasting tin with hot oil and roast at 200C for 50 minutes, turning once if you remember. They want to be eaten within 10 minutes of leaving the oven or they lose their crunch.

Pan gravy
When the chicken comes out to rest, pour off most of the fat from the roasting tin. Put the tin on the hob, stir in 2 tbsp flour and cook for 2 minutes, then whisk in 500 ml stock bit by bit. Simmer gently for 10 minutes, stirring now and then (you don't need to stand over it). Strain into a jug - 2 minutes. Keeps warm for 15 minutes.

Buttered greens
Shred half a savoy cabbage (5 min, can be done hours ahead). Boil the cabbage with a mug of frozen peas for 4 minutes, drain, toss with butter and pepper, 1 minute. Serve straight away - they go grey if they sit more than 5 minutes.
