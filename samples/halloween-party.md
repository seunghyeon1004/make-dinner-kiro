Halloween dinner party for six

== Pumpkin soup in mugs ==
Cut a 1.2 kg pumpkin into wedges, scoop the seeds, toss with oil and salt (10 minutes). Roast the wedges for 40 minutes in a hot oven until soft and catching at the edges. While they roast, soften a chopped onion and two garlic cloves in butter in a big pot, about 8 minutes, stirring. Scoop the roasted flesh into the pot, add 1 litre of stock and simmer 15 minutes. Blend until smooth with a stick blender (3 minutes). It will sit happily on the lowest heat for 30 minutes. Ladle into mugs at the last moment, 3 minutes.

== Sticky blood-orange drumsticks ==
The marinade: juice of 2 blood oranges, 3 tbsp soy, 2 tbsp honey, grated ginger. Whisk it (5 min) and turn 12 drumsticks in it. Leave to marinate for at least 30 minutes; they can sit in it all afternoon. Bake on a lined tray for 45 minutes, basting halfway. Rest 5 minutes. Fine to wait 15 minutes before serving.

== Midnight rice ==
Rinse 400 g black rice (2 min). Put in a pan with 800 ml water and a pinch of salt, bring to the boil then cover and simmer on low for 35 minutes. Don't lift the lid. Off the heat, leave it covered to steam for 10 minutes, then fluff with a fork (1 minute). Stays hot in the covered pan for 20 minutes.

== Witch-finger breadsticks ==
Mix 300 g flour, a sachet of yeast, salt, 200 ml warm water and a glug of oil into a dough and knead for 8 minutes. Let it rise for 45 minutes somewhere warm. Roll into 16 knobbly fingers and press an almond into the tip of each for the nail - 12 minutes. Bake 15 minutes until golden. They're best warm, within 20 minutes of baking.

== Caramel apples ==
(make these first, they need to set)
Push sticks into 6 small apples, 3 minutes. Melt 200 g sugar with 60 ml water in a small pan and cook without stirring until it is a deep amber caramel, about 12 minutes - watch it the whole time, it turns fast. Dip the apples and stand them on baking paper (5 minutes). Leave to set for 30 minutes. They keep all evening.
