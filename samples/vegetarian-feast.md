MUSHROOM WELLINGTON
Finely chop 600 g mushrooms and 2 shallots (12 minutes, or less with a food processor). Cook them in a wide pan over a high heat, stirring often, until all the liquid has gone and they are dark and dry - 15 minutes. Stir in thyme and a spoon of mustard and let the mixture cool completely, 30 minutes; it can be made the day before. Unroll a sheet of puff pastry, spread the filling down the middle, fold over, seal, brush with beaten egg and score the top: 10 minutes. Bake at 200C for 35 minutes until deep golden. Rest 10 minutes before slicing so it holds together; slice at the table. It is fine for up to 15 minutes after resting.

MAPLE ROAST CARROTS
Peel 800 g carrots and halve them lengthways (8 minutes). Toss with oil, maple syrup and salt (1 minute). Roast at 200C for 30 minutes. Good for 15 minutes out of the oven.

GARLIC MASH
Peel and chop 1.2 kg potatoes, 10 minutes. Boil with 4 whole garlic cloves for 20 minutes until completely tender. Drain and let them steam dry in the colander for 3 minutes. Mash with butter and warm milk, 5 minutes. Covered, it stays hot for 20 minutes.

ONION GRAVY
Slice 3 onions (5 minutes). Cook slowly in butter for 25 minutes until soft and brown; stir every few minutes but you can do other things. Add a spoon of flour, cook 1 minute stirring, then pour in 500 ml vegetable stock and a splash of soy and simmer for 10 minutes. Keeps hot for 20 minutes.

GREEN BEANS WITH ALMONDS
Top and tail 400 g green beans (5 minutes, do it ahead). Toast a handful of flaked almonds in a dry pan for 3 minutes, shaking the pan - they keep. Boil the beans 4 minutes, drain, toss with butter and the almonds (1 minute). Serve within 5 minutes.
