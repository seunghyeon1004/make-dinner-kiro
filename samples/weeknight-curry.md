Chickpea and spinach curry
- chop 1 onion, 3 cloves garlic, thumb of ginger (6 min)
- fry the onion in oil until golden, 8 min, stirring
- add garlic, ginger and 2 tbsp curry powder, fry 1 min
- add 2 tins chickpeas and 1 tin tomatoes, simmer 20 min, lid off, stir once or twice
- stir in 200 g spinach until wilted, 2 min. Holds on low heat for 20 min.

Basmati rice
Rinse 300 g rice until the water runs clear, 3 minutes. Bring to the boil with 450 ml water, then lid on, lowest heat, 12 minutes. Take off the heat and leave covered for 10 minutes. Fluff. Stays hot under the lid for 15 minutes.

Cucumber raita
Grate half a cucumber and squeeze out the water. Stir into 250 g yoghurt with chopped mint and a pinch of salt. 5 minutes all in, and it keeps in the fridge.

Quick flatbreads
Stir 250 g self-raising flour into 250 g yoghurt with a pinch of salt and bring together into a dough - 5 minutes. Rest the dough 15 minutes. Divide into 6 and roll out thin (6 minutes). Cook in a dry, very hot frying pan, about 2 minutes a side, in batches: 12 minutes in total and you have to stand there. Eat within 10 minutes.
