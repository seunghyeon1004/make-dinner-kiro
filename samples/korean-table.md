A Korean home dinner for four. Everything goes on the table at once.

Steamed rice (bap)
Wash 3 cups of short-grain rice, changing the water three or four times, 4 minutes. Soak for 30 minutes. Drain, add 3 cups of fresh water, bring to the boil in a heavy pot, then cover and cook on the lowest heat for 15 minutes. Turn off the heat and let it steam, lid on, for 10 minutes. Stays hot in the pot for 25 minutes.

Doenjang-jjigae (soybean paste stew)
Cut 1 courgette, 1 onion, 1 potato and a block of tofu into bite-sized cubes: 8 minutes, can be done ahead. Bring 700 ml anchovy stock to the boil and dissolve 3 tbsp doenjang in it, 5 minutes. Add the potato and onion and simmer for 10 minutes. Add the courgette, tofu and a sliced chilli and simmer 6 minutes more. Holds on a low flame for 15 minutes.

Bulgogi
Mix the marinade (soy sauce, grated pear, sugar, garlic, sesame oil, black pepper), 6 minutes. Toss 600 g thinly sliced beef in it and marinate for 30 minutes; longer is fine, up to overnight. Stir-fry in a very hot pan in two batches, 8 minutes altogether, standing at the stove. Serve within 5 minutes - it toughens as it cools.

Sigeumchi-namul (seasoned spinach)
Blanch 300 g spinach in boiling water for 1 minute. Rinse in cold water and squeeze dry, 3 minutes. Dress with soy sauce, garlic, sesame oil and sesame seeds, 2 minutes. Served at room temperature; it keeps.

Dubu-buchim (pan-fried tofu)
Slice a block of firm tofu and pat the slices dry, 4 minutes. Fry in a little oil until golden on both sides, about 10 minutes, turning once. Spoon over a soy-and-spring-onion sauce (2 minutes to mix, any time). Good for 15 minutes.
